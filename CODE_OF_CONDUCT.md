# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Open Grid contributors and maintainers must make participation respectful,
professional, and free from harassment. Constructive disagreement about code and
design is welcome; personal attacks, discriminatory language, intimidation,
unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- discussing technical tradeoffs with evidence and clear reasoning
- respecting different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints
- accepting feedback and correcting mistakes
- keeping public and private project spaces focused on the project

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, reviews,
project communication spaces, and when representing Open Grid publicly.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/Goatshave/open-grid/security/advisories/new).
Do not expose sensitive personal information in a public issue. Maintainers will
review reports as promptly and impartially as practical, protect reporter privacy
where possible, and may remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently ban participation depending on severity and pattern.

Maintainers who are the subject of a report must not participate in deciding its
outcome. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is a
violation of this policy.
